5. Asset 2: Completed Observation Document (Executive Summary)

The observation document (docs/observation-document.md) provides a rigorous technical retrospective across all 6 required areas:

1. Production Deployment & Readiness Assessment

Containerized using Docker and orchestrated for AWS ECS Fargate with KMS encryption and ALB. The architecture enforces an inviolable separation: all access control, date math, and money calculations execute deterministically before the LLM pipeline is invoked.

2. Evaluation Suite Results (150 Cases)

Evaluated on 150 diverse synthetic claims: 49 Approvals, 40 Denials, 32 Referrals (Fraud), and 29 Routing cases. Crucially, the suite includes 33 hostile adversarial attacks across 4 distinct threat families: prompt injection (8), cross-record leakage (9), forced auto-decision (8), and narrative contradictions (8). The pipeline achieved 100% defense with zero unauthorized data exposure.

3. Observability, Telemetry & SLA Compliance

Instrumented with an in-memory & CloudWatch metrics pipeline tracking invocation volume, error rate, and p50/p95/p99 latencies. Configured with automated CloudWatch alarms (HighLatencyP95Alarm > 60s, HighErrorRateAlarm > 5%). Average local p95 latency is 1,570 ms—substantially outperforming the 60,000 ms SLA.

4. Platform Reliability & Failure Modes (SEV-2 Incident Drill)

Conducted a live regression drill simulating an upstream model timeout breach. The CloudWatch alarm triggered within 30 seconds, initiating an automated ECS task rollback. Full service recovery was verified with a Mean Time to Resolution (MTTR) of 2 minutes 45 seconds (detailed in docs/incident-report.md).

5. Operational Handover & Production Runbook

Authored 4 complete Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) in docs/runbook.md enabling independent operators to run the system: SOP-01 (Provisioning Users), SOP-02 (Rolling Back Deployments), SOP-03 (Escalating to SIU), and SOP-04 (Updating Policy Corpus & Re-indexing BM25).

6. Engineering Retrospective & Lessons Learned

Key takeaways include: (a) deterministic spines eliminate financial hallucinations; (b) smaller foundation models require strict prompt sanitization and JSON fence stripping; and (c) human-in-the-loop write-back is the most effective safeguard for high-stakes insurance adjudication.
